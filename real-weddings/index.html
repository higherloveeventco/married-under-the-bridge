Real weddings

Days we've spent under this bridge.

It's easier to picture your own day when you can see somebody else's. This is one afternoon at Cathedral Park: the staircase under the arches, the picnic once it's set, and the live painting this couple added on.

On the staircase

Under the arches.

The staircase beneath the St. Johns Bridge, and the portraits afterward. Florals throughout by Still City Flowers.

Waiting when you get back

The picnic reception.

Linen, candlelight, mismatched vintage china, a grazing spread from She Serves and Oregon wine poured into cut crystal. All of it set while you are off having portraits taken.

The rest of the afternoon

Sweets and games.

Desserts from Flower & Flour, Oregon sparkling wine, and bocce and checkers out on the grass. All of this is included in The Garden Party and The Soirée.

Add-on · $1,800

Live event painting.

This one is an add-on rather than part of a package. Yoonie Mae sets up at the reception and paints through the afternoon while your guests add to it, and you take the canvas home. It isn't included in any tier, so choose it when you book if you want it.
