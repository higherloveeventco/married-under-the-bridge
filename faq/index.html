The basics

What this is, what it costs, and what you get.

What is Married Under the Bridge?
An all-inclusive wedding day at Cathedral Park, from the ceremony to the last plate cleared.

A legal ceremony beneath the St. Johns Bridge, portraits in Cathedral Park while we set the tables, then a styled picnic reception where your people join you. It runs from 3:00 PM to 7:30 PM.

It's a collaboration between two St. Johns businesses. Weddinglandia handles the ceremony, the officiant, and your marriage license filing. Higher Love handles the reception, the vendor team, the permits, and everything on the day.

How much does it cost?
$2,500 for the two of you. $3,600 with up to 8 guests. $5,000 with up to 18.

All three are genuinely all-inclusive. Ceremony, officiant, license filing, photography, florals, charcuterie, desserts, beverages, permit fees, linens, place settings, setup and teardown are all in the price.

The only things you pay for separately are your marriage license fee, which goes to the county, and personal florals if you want them. Rings, what you wear, hair and makeup, and travel are yours as well.

A typical Garden Party wedding with a bouquet and boutonniere comes to $3,855 all in. The full breakdown is on the packages page.

What is actually included?
Everything except your license, personal florals, rings, attire, and travel.
  • Legal ceremony with an ordained officiant, ceremony recording, and marriage license filing
  • Photography, with a sneak peek in 48 hours and the full gallery in four to six weeks
  • A styled picnic reception with linens, place settings, votives, blankets and cushions
  • Charcuterie and grazing from She Serves, with vegan options
  • Desserts from Flower & Flour
  • Fresh floral table arrangements from Still City Flowers
  • A beverage allowance for Oregon wine and North Portland beer, plus seasonally infused water
  • Your Portland Parks permit fee
  • A Bluetooth speaker on your playlist, and for the two larger packages, bocce, cornhole and a bubble send-off
  • All setup, styling and teardown
Where exactly does it happen?
Cathedral Park in St. Johns, North Portland, beneath the St. Johns Bridge.

About fifteen minutes from downtown and thirty from the airport. It's a public park, free to visit, and open the whole time you're there.

The picnic reception is in a quieter, more tucked-away part of the park that we reserve for you. The ceremony is either at the staircase beneath the arches or at a spot we reserve nearby, which is your choice. The ceremony section explains both.

How long does the day last?
Three hours for Just the Two of Us. Four and a half for the other two.

The Garden Party and The Soirée run 3:00 PM to 7:30 PM. Ceremony at 3:00, portraits until 4:30, then the picnic. Just the Two of Us is the same shape, shorter.

Times are measured from the scheduled start, so arriving late shortens your day rather than extending it. We can't add time on the day.

Is the marriage legally binding?
Yes. Fully legal, with an ordained officiant and your license filed for you.

You bring a valid Oregon marriage license, your officiant performs the ceremony, and Weddinglandia files the paperwork afterward. It's a wedding, not a symbolic one.

If you turn up without a valid license we can still hold the ceremony, but it won't be legal and the package isn't refundable, so please build the license into your timeline.

Do you marry LGBTQIA+ couples?
Yes, gladly, and we'd be glad to marry you.

Higher Love is woman-owned, and we welcome couples of every background and orientation. Our add-ons include two bouquets and two boutonnieres precisely because not every couple needs one of each.

The ceremony

Where you say your vows, and who gets to be there.

How many people can come to the ceremony?
Five at the staircase. Everyone in your package at a reserved spot in the park. It's your choice.

The staircase beneath the arches is one of two locations Portland Parks treats as an official wedding venue, and reserving it takes a wedding permit that only you can apply for. Without one you can still marry there, but only as a simple ceremony of five: the two of you, your officiant, and two witnesses. Everyone counts toward that five, children included, and witnesses have to be 18 or over. It isn't something we can flex.

Everywhere else in Cathedral Park is covered by the special use permit we already hold for your reception, and that permit allows up to 30 people at a ceremony. That's more than any of our packages hold, so your whole guest list can be there, children included. There's no seating, so guests stand, and then you all walk to the picnic together.

You tell us which one 10 days before your date. Both options are laid out here.

Can you guarantee the staircase?
No. It can't be reserved through us, and from 2027 another wedding may hold it.

Without a wedding permit the staircase is first come, first served on the day. If someone else is standing there at 3:00 PM, we move to our reserved spot in the park and carry on. That isn't grounds for a refund or a reschedule, which is why we'd rather you know now than find out then.

From 2027, Portland Parks is bringing back full wedding permits, so couples will be able to reserve the staircase in advance. On any given 2027 date, someone else may hold it.

If having a confirmed location matters to you, choose the reserved spot in the park. You'll still be under the bridge and in the photographs.

Can we get the staircase guaranteed if we really want it?
Yes, but only by getting a Portland Parks wedding permit yourselves.

That permit has to be in your name. Portland Parks will only deal with the person named on it, so we can't apply for it, hold it, or stand in line for you.

  • It can't be requested by phone, email or online. You have to attend in person.
  • Reservations open on one specific day each January, first come first served, and Cathedral Park is expected to be in very high demand.
  • The staircase usage fee is $683.75 for up to 100 people, plus a non-refundable application fee of $133.50 if you book more than 45 days ahead. Around $817 in total.
  • Fees are set by Portland Parks and can change, so confirm current pricing with them.

If you hold that permit, your whole guest list can attend the ceremony on the staircase. Tell us you're going for it and we'll explain exactly what to expect.

Who are our witnesses?
Two of your guests, or Weddinglandia can provide them.

Witnesses have to be 18 or over. If you're eloping alone, or you'd simply rather not ask anyone, Weddinglandia will provide two and it's entirely normal.

Can we write our own vows?
Yes. Weddinglandia will work the ceremony around whatever you want to say.

Personal vows, traditional vows, readings, a moment for someone who isn't there. Raise it with Jasmine's team and they'll build the ceremony around it. There are only a few lines that are legally required.

Is there a rehearsal?
No, and you won't need one.

The ceremony is short and your officiant walks you through exactly what happens on the day, before it starts. Nobody has ever needed a practice run.

What if we're running late?
Tell us as soon as you know. The ceremony gets shorter rather than the day getting longer.

Your package covers a fixed window, and vendors have other commitments, so we can't extend the end time. If you arrive late your officiant may shorten the ceremony script, except the parts required by law, to keep everything on schedule.

Your guests

How many, who counts, and when they should arrive.

How many guests can we have in total?
Ten people for The Garden Party, twenty for The Soirée, counting the two of you.

The Garden Party is the two of you plus 8. The Soirée is the two of you plus 18.

You can add up to four more people to either at $75 each, taking The Garden Party to 14 and The Soirée to 24. Added guests sit in the blanket picnic area rather than at a table, because table seating can't be extended past what your package includes. Their beverage allowance goes up $25 per person.

Twenty-four total is our hard ceiling and it isn't something we can waive. Over 14 people you'll need The Soirée, because that's the package with the second table.

Do children count toward our guest count?
Three and over, yes. Under three, no.

Children under three aren't given a table seat or a place setting and are expected to be held or seated with a parent. Everyone three and older counts as a guest and gets their own seat.

At a staircase ceremony, children count toward the five-person limit like everyone else.

When should we tell our guests to arrive?
4:30 PM if you've chosen the staircase ceremony. Earlier if you've chosen the reserved spot.

For a staircase ceremony, put Reception at 4:30 PM, Cathedral Park on your invitations and leave the ceremony time off entirely. It saves a lot of explaining and means nobody turns up early hoping to watch. 3:00 PM is your ceremony time and it's just for the five of you.

If you've chosen a reserved spot in the park, your guests are invited to the ceremony. We'll confirm the arrival time with you and you can put that on the invitations instead.

Can guests arrive early anyway?
Please don't. Ask them to arrive at the time you've given them.

If they're not attending the ceremony, guests who arrive early will be asked to wait away from the ceremony area. It's not us being strict for its own sake. The first ninety minutes are yours, and the moment they see is you walking up already married.

Where do guests park?
Free street parking around the park, and a lot off N Edison Street.

Cathedral Park is residential and parking is generally straightforward, though busier on warm weekends. Tell your guests to allow ten minutes and to walk down into the park rather than driving in.

Booking and paying

How to book, what you pay when, and what happens if plans change.

How do we book?
Online, in one sitting. No inquiry form and no waiting to hear back.

Pick your date and package, choose any add-ons and your beverage preference, sign, and pay your deposit. It all happens in the same flow. Book here.

If you'd rather talk to a person first, that's completely fine. Book a free 20 minute call with Kylie and she'll go through it with you. No pressure and no sales pitch.

When do we have to pay, and how much?
A deposit at booking, then the balance 7 days before your wedding.

Deposits are $1,000 for Just the Two of Us, $1,500 for The Garden Party, and $2,100 for The Soirée. Your date isn't held until the deposit arrives.

There's no schedule between those two points. Pay the balance in one go at the end, or send whatever you like whenever you like and chip away at it. We'll always tell you where you stand if you ask.

Can we get a refund if we change our minds?
No. Deposits and payments aren't refundable or transferable if you cancel.

The deposit books eight or nine independent vendors for your date and turns away other couples, which is why we can't return it. We also don't offer credit toward a future booking.

There are two situations outside your control where you do get your money back, and one where we'll reschedule. The next answer covers those.

Is there anything you would refund?
Yes. If Portland Parks cancels on you, or if no 2027 date works out.
  • Portland Parks declares your date unavailable, or withdraws or refuses a permit for reasons that aren't your fault. You choose: reschedule, or a full refund of everything you've paid.
  • You reserved a 2027 date and nothing on the calendar suits you. You can withdraw and we refund your deposit in full.

Separately, we'll reschedule you once within 12 months if conditions make travel or being outdoors genuinely unsafe, like a declared weather emergency or road closures. Rain, cloud and cold don't count.

Can we change our package after booking?
Usually yes, upward. Ask us as early as you can.

Moving up a tier is normally fine if the vendor team can accommodate it. It gets harder close to the date, and once the park permit application has gone in, any increase in numbers needs Portland Parks to agree and may not be possible.

Everything is locked 10 days before your date.

What if we need to reschedule?
Only where conditions make travel or being outdoors genuinely unsafe.

A declared weather emergency, road closures, that kind of thing. In those cases you get one reschedule within 12 months, subject to the whole vendor team being free on the new date.

Rain, cloud, wind or a cold afternoon aren't grounds for rescheduling. We have a rain plan for exactly that. All requests need to be in writing to hello@higherloveeventco.com.

Can we see the terms before we book?
Yes. Email us and we'll send them.

We'd genuinely rather you read them first than be surprised later. Ask us for a copy and we'll send the whole thing. They're also in the booking flow before you pay anything.

Dates and 2027

What's bookable now, and how a 2027 wedding works.

Can we request a date that isn't listed?
Yes. The listed dates are the instantly bookable ones, not the only ones.

Email us or click Join Waitlist on our booking page with the date you have in mind and we'll check it against the vendor team.

Give us as much notice as you can. Availability depends on eight or nine people, not just us.

Can we book a date in 2027?
Yes, but you'll pick the exact date in January 2027 rather than now.

Portland Parks publishes the list of dates the park is closed to private events in January of each year. Until that comes out, nobody knows which 2027 dates are unavailable, including us.

You have two ways to go:

  • Reserve now. You book using January 1, 2027 as a placeholder, which locks today's pricing and holds the vendor team. In January we look at the calendar together and choose your real date, and we update your booking. Nothing about your package or price changes. If nothing on the calendar works for you, you can withdraw and we refund your deposit in full.
  • Wait and book in January. You see the calendar first and decide with full information. The tradeoff is that another couple may book the team for your date before you do.

Neither is wrong. The first protects your pricing and your team. The second gives you certainty about the date before you commit anything.

Are any 2027 dates already ruled out?
Yes. July 16 to 18, 2027 is the Cathedral Park Jazz Festival.

The festival takes over the entire park and Portland Parks doesn't issue event permits during it. Those three days are unavailable and there's no way around it.

For reference, these dates were blocked in 2026. They aren't confirmed for 2027, but the pattern tends to repeat: June 7, June 28, July 12, July 16 to 20, and July 25. If you're drawn to mid-July, that's the stretch most likely to go again.

Do you do winter weddings?
Yes. From November the ceremony stays under the bridge and the reception moves indoors.

The Weddinglandia chapel is two blocks from the park. Same ceremony, same photography, same food and florals, in a warm room instead of on a lawn.

Email us for winter dates and pricing.

Permits and paperwork

The two things only you can do, and everything we handle.

What do we actually have to do ourselves?
Two things: get your marriage license, and submit one short park permit application.

That's the whole list. Everything else is ours.

The permit has to be in your name because Portland Parks will only communicate with the person named on it. We prepare the application, walk you through it on a ten minute call after you book, and remind you before the deadline.

The full timeline is here, with when each thing happens.

Yours to do: two things
Why can't you submit the permit for us?
Portland Parks requires the permit holder to be the host, and the host is you.

They'll only correspond with the person named on the permit. It also means you're the host of your own event, which is what allows alcohol to be there at all.

We give you everything you need, including all the event details, and we handle every part of the process after your permit number comes through, including paying the fee. The fee is already in your package price.

When is the permit application due?
21 days before your date, and earlier is better.

Portland Parks recommends at least 30 days. If you book fewer than 21 days out, the application needs to go in within 24 hours. Less notice than they'd like means approval is at their discretion and rush fees may apply, which would be added to your final invoice.

If the application doesn't go in on time, or isn't approved, your reception moves indoors to the Weddinglandia chapel that same day at no extra cost. Nothing is refunded in that case, which is why it's worth doing early. We'll remind you.

When should we get our marriage license?
About two months before the wedding, and no later than a week out.

An Oregon license takes three days to become valid and expires 60 days after it's issued, so there's a window rather than a deadline.

It costs $60 in Multnomah County and most Oregon counties, or $65 if you ask to waive the three day wait. Any county clerk's office in Oregon works, it doesn't have to be Multnomah, and you both need to attend in person with photo ID.

If you're booking a 2027 date, don't get it early. It would expire long before your wedding.

Yours to do
Can we use an out-of-state marriage license?
No. It has to be an Oregon license.

The ceremony takes place in Oregon, so an Oregon license is what makes it legal. Any Oregon county can issue it regardless of where you live.

Food and drink

What's on the table, and how alcohol works in a public park.

What food is included?
A charcuterie and grazing spread from She Serves, plus desserts from Flower & Flour.

Cheeses, cured meats, fruits, nuts and breads, arranged across the table among the florals and candles rather than confined to a board. The Soirée gets an expanded spread.

The Garden Party and The Soirée also get a separately styled dessert table, so the sweets have a home of their own and the main table stays comfortable.

Can you cater for dietary needs?
Yes. Vegan options are available and allergies are worked around. Tell us 10 days out.

One honest caveat: the food is prepared in kitchens that handle nuts, dairy, gluten and other common allergens, so we can't guarantee against cross-contamination and nothing is described as allergen-free. If a guest has a severe allergy, please tell us and also tell them.

How do beer and wine work?
Your package includes an allowance and we buy the drinks for you at cost, no markup.

The allowance is $75 for Just the Two of Us, $225 for The Garden Party, and $450 for The Soirée. It goes up $25 for each additional guest you add. It covers beer, wine and champagne.

Because the park permit is in your name, you're the host and the alcohol is yours. We buy it on your behalf, at cost with receipts available, and hand it over sealed for you to serve. We don't pour or serve alcohol at any point, so you'll want to nominate someone to open bottles.

Park rules: no glass anywhere, so cans or plastic only, and no spirits.

The allowance is a budget rather than a fixed charge. Spend less and the difference is credited back. Spend more and the difference is invoiced, agreed with you in writing beforehand.

What if we don't drink?
Choose non-alcoholic at booking and the full allowance is credited to your final invoice.

That's $75, $225 or $450 off depending on your package, plus any increase from additional guests. Seasonally infused water from a dispenser is included either way.

Can our guests bring their own drinks?
Please keep to what's arranged through us.

You're the permit holder, which makes you responsible for everything consumed at your event. Sticking to the arranged beverages keeps that clean. If you want more than the allowance covers, tell us at least 14 days ahead and we'll sort it.

No glass and no spirits, whatever the source. Those are park rules rather than ours.

Is there a cake?
Not included, but available as an add-on in three sizes.

A cutting cake from Flower & Flour: $75 serves 2, $105 serves 10, $165 serves 20. The dessert spread is included regardless and already has cookies, brownies and cupcakes in it.

On the day

What the setup looks like and what to expect from a public park.

What does the picnic setup actually look like?
Linen-draped picnic tables with florals, candles and place settings, and blankets on the lawn.

We reserve a quieter, tucked-away corner of the park. The Garden Party includes one picnic table seating up to 10. The Soirée includes two. Both get a third table styled separately for desserts.

Blankets and cushions go on the lawn for anyone who'd rather settle closer to the ground. We use tables rather than a ground spread as the main setup because we think every guest should be comfortable and included.

Will there be other people in the park?
Yes. Cathedral Park stays open to the public throughout.

Expect walkers, dogs, and someone else admiring the bridge. We can't close the park and wouldn't want to. In practice it's part of the charm, and the spot we reserve is one of the quieter ones.

Can we have music?
A Bluetooth speaker on your playlist, at conversational volume.

Send us the playlist and it's ready when you arrive. Amplified sound is restricted by Portland Parks rules, so a live band or a DJ isn't possible in the park.

Can we have a sparkler send-off?
No sparklers, but bubbles are included for The Garden Party and The Soirée.

Sparklers aren't permitted in the park. Bubbles photograph better anyway, and nobody has to hold a lit thing near a wedding dress.

Is there anything to do besides eat?
Bocce and cornhole, set up and ready, for the two larger packages.

Plus the park itself, the river, and a neighbourhood full of places to carry on afterward. We'll send you a guide if you tell us where your guests are coming from.

Do we need to clean up?
No. We pack out everything we brought in.

The one thing that's yours to take is any unopened alcohol, since it's legally yours rather than ours.

Weather

What happens when Portland does what Portland does.

What happens if it rains?
The picnic moves indoors to the Weddinglandia chapel, two blocks away, at no extra cost.

Your ceremony, food, florals, photography and everything else in your package stays exactly the same. It's a real backup rather than a fallback.

We make that call by 10:00 AM on the day and tell you straight away, so you're never standing in the park wondering what's happening.

What counts as too wet?
More than light rain. A drizzle isn't a problem.

This package is built for Portland, and a grey sky or a light shower is part of the experience rather than something to solve. A staircase ceremony has the bridge overhead as natural cover. If we're using a reserved spot in the park, we'll pick the location with the best shelter for the conditions.

Can we cancel or move the date because of weather?
Only if conditions are genuinely unsafe, not just unpleasant.

A declared weather emergency, road closures, dangerous air quality or heavy snow would qualify, and you'd get one reschedule within 12 months subject to vendor availability.

Rain, cloud, wind or a cold afternoon wouldn't. That's what the indoor plan is for.

Accessibility

What the park is actually like to get around.

Is Cathedral Park accessible?
Mostly yes, with one exception: the staircase is not ADA accessible.

The park has paved, accessible paths and accessible restrooms. The picnic area we reserve is reachable on paved path, and everyone sits at picnic tables rather than on the ground, so no guest has to get down to a blanket to be included.

The staircase and deck beneath the bridge is the exception. If anyone in your group uses a wheelchair or a walker, or finds stairs difficult, choose the reserved ceremony spot in the park instead. It's on level ground, and it's also the option where all your guests can attend the ceremony.

The permanent restrooms sit lower in the park, a few minutes' walk from the picnic area.

We have specific access needs. Who do we tell?
Tell us at booking, or whenever you know. Earlier means we can plan around it.

Email hello@higherloveeventco.com or raise it on a call. We'd much rather build the day around what your group needs than have you find out on the afternoon that something doesn't work.

One thing we can't control: the park is public and Portland Parks maintains it, so we can't guarantee the state of the facilities on any given day.

Photos and keepsakes

What you get, and when.

How much photography is included?
One hour for Just the Two of Us. Two hours for the other two packages.

Just the Two of Us covers 30 minutes of ceremony and 30 minutes of couple portraits. The Garden Party and The Soirée cover 30 minutes of ceremony, an hour of couple portraits, and 30 minutes of guest candids.

When do we get our photos?
A sneak peek within 48 hours, the full gallery in four to six weeks.

The sneak peek is a small set of edited images, delivered digitally, so you have something to share almost straight away.

What's the ceremony recording?
A simple recording of the ceremony itself, included with every package.

It's documentation rather than a wedding film, so treat it as a keepsake rather than a substitute for the photography. If you want proper video, digital and Super 8 are both available as add-ons.

Can we opt out of you using our photos?
Yes. Tell us in writing before the day and we won't use them.

By default we and our vendors may use images, video and artwork from your day for portfolio, website and social media. Email us before your date and that's the end of it, no explanation needed.
