How the day flows

Vows, Portraits, and a Picnic.

Every wedding starts at 3:00 PM, which puts your portraits in the best light of the day and your picnic in the warm part of the afternoon. The times below are The Garden Party and The Soirée. Just the Two of Us follows the same shape an hour and a half shorter, finishing at 6:00.

01 3:00 to 3:30 PM

Ceremony at Cathedral Park

Thirty minutes beneath the bridge with an ordained officiant from Weddinglandia. Personal vows or traditional ones, whichever you want. They record the ceremony for you, sign your license on the day, and file it with the county afterward.

You choose where it happens, and that's what decides who can be there. Both options are below.

02 3:30 to 4:30 PM

Portraits in the park

While we set the picnic, you and your photographer walk the park. An hour with just the two of you and a camera, whether your guests were at the ceremony or are still on their way.

03 4:30 to 7:30 PM

The picnic awaits

The picnic opens when your portraits are done. Linen-draped tables, blankets, cushions and trays out on the lawn, florals, candles, a full spread from She Serves and desserts from Flower & Flour. If your guests came to the ceremony they walk over with you. If they didn't, they're waiting here at 4:30 to welcome you as newlyweds. Either way you sit down and enjoy it.

Where you say your vows

One question to answer.

There are two places in the park we hold ceremonies. The stone staircase that runs up to the bridge, under the gothic arches, which is the picture everyone has in their head. Or the open lawn below, where everybody you love gets to watch. Tell us which 10 days before your date and we'll take it from there.

Children are welcome and count toward your guest number. Nobody sits, so everyone stands for the vows, and then you all walk over to the picnic together.

2027 dates aren't up yet, and that isn't us dragging our feet. Portland Parks publishes the list of days the park is closed to private events every January, so the 2027 list won't exist until January 2027. Nobody knows those dates yet, us included.

Good to know

Questions couples ask us.

How many people can come to the ceremony?

Five on the staircase, or everyone in your package on the park lawn. It's your call, and you tell us 10 days before the date.

The staircase can't be reserved, which is why it's capped at the two of you, your officiant and two witnesses. The lawn is a spot we hold for you, and it takes your whole guest list, standing. You can have everyone on the staircase too, but that takes a separate wedding permit at extra cost, which you apply for yourselves. Both options, side by side.

What do we actually have to do ourselves?

Two things. Get your Oregon marriage license, and be on one phone call about a month before your date.

Every booking needs a park permit, and Portland Parks will only issue it to the person named on it. So we call them together, you do the talking and applying, I'm there to answer anything, and once it's in you hang up and I pay the fee. About fifteen minutes, and the fee is included in your package price. Every step is here.

What happens if it rains?

The picnic moves indoors to the Weddinglandia chapel, two blocks from the park, at no extra cost. Your ceremony, food, florals and everything else stays the same.

We decide by 10:00 AM on the day and tell you right then, so you're never standing in the park wondering. Light rain isn't a reason to move.

Can we book a date for 2027?

Yes, though you'll pick the exact date in January 2027 rather than now. Portland Parks doesn't publish which days the park is closed until January of that year.

You can reserve now with a January 1, 2027 placeholder. That locks today's pricing, holds the team, and puts you in the queue, so when the calendar publishes you get first pick ahead of anyone who waited.
