The experience

A Cathedral Park wedding experience.
A collaboration of St. Johns creatives.

Higher Love Event Co. and Weddinglandia both call St. Johns home. This package is what happens when local businesses choose collaboration and community over competition.

Weddinglandia coordinates the ceremony. Higher Love coordinates the picnic reception for you and your favorite people, the vendor team, and all logistics. Wedding permits beneath the St. Johns Bridge are currently unavailable while repairs wrap up, but between the two of us we found a way to help you get Married Under the Bridge.

The rest of the day belongs to our neighbors. The photography, the florals, the charcuterie, the desserts, the wine and the beer all come from St. Johns and North Portland businesses — people we know by name and have worked with directly. One neighborhood, showing up for your wedding day.

Where you say your vows

Two ways to do the ceremony.

Cathedral Park has two spots that Portland Parks treats as official wedding venues, and the staircase is one of them. Booking either takes a wedding permit that only you can apply for. The rest of the park works differently, and that difference is what gives you a choice. You tell us which one 10 days before your date.

Option one

The staircase.

The spot beneath the arches, and one of the two locations Portland Parks treats as an official wedding venue. Reserving it takes a wedding permit, which isn't part of your package and which only you can apply for.

Without that permit you can still marry there, but only as a simple ceremony: five people, on a first come, first served basis. Everyone else waits at the picnic and sees you walk up married, which is its own kind of moment.

If someone else is on the staircase at 3:00, we move to our reserved spot in the park and carry on.

Five people: the two of you, your officiant, and two witnesses. Witnesses must be 18 or older. Everyone counts, including children. Guests arrive at 4:30.
Option two

A reserved spot in the park.

Everywhere in Cathedral Park outside those two official venues is covered by the special use permit we already hold for your reception. So we choose a spot with the bridge overhead, confirm it in advance, and it is genuinely yours for the afternoon.

That permit allows up to 30 people at the ceremony, which is more than any of our packages hold. So everyone on your guest list can be there, children included. There is no seating, so your guests stand, and then you all walk over to the picnic together.

Everyone in your package. Guests arrive for the ceremony.

From 2027, Portland Parks is bringing back full wedding permits, which means couples will be able to reserve the staircase in advance and another wedding may hold it on your date. We cannot apply for that permit on your behalf. If a confirmed location and having everyone present matter more to you than the staircase itself, option two is the one we would point you toward.

Planning a 2027 wedding?

2027 dates aren't listed yet, and that isn't us being slow. Portland Parks publishes the list of dates the park is closed to private events in January of each year, so the 2027 list won't exist until January 2027. Nobody knows those dates yet, us included.

If you're looking that far out, you have two ways to go:

  • Reserve now, choose your date in January. You book using January 1, 2027 as a placeholder, which locks today's pricing and holds the vendor team for you. When the calendar publishes, we pick your real date together and update your booking. Nothing about your package or price changes. If nothing on the calendar works for you, you can withdraw and we refund your deposit in full.
  • Wait and book in January. You see the calendar first and decide with full information. The tradeoff is that another couple may book the team for your date before you do.

One date we already know is out: July 16 through 18, 2027 is the Cathedral Park Jazz Festival, and the park is closed to events that weekend.

The staircase also works differently from 2027 onward, which we've explained in the ceremony options above. Email us and we'll walk you through all of it.

The neighborhood

Make a weekend of it in St. Johns.

St. Johns is a small town that happens to sit inside Portland — a main street of breweries, wine bars, bakeries, and shops, a few minutes from the park. Your wedding is one afternoon, but the neighborhood is worth more than that.

The night before

Gather everyone early.

A rehearsal dinner, drinks, or just a table big enough for whoever made the trip. St. Johns has room for a group without the reservation gymnastics of downtown, and it puts your people in the neighborhood before the day begins.

The wedding day

The neighborhood comes to you.

Wine from 45th Parallel. Beer from StormBreaker Brewing and Occidental Brewing Company, brewed within a few minutes of the park. The flowers, the grazing spread, the desserts, the photography — all from businesses in St. Johns and North Portland. Every part of your table has a name and an address attached to it.

After

Keep the night going.

The park closes out in the early evening, but St. Johns doesn't. Walk up for dinner or a nightcap with whoever's still with you. Plenty of couples make it a tradition and come back on their anniversary.

Tell us where your guests are coming from and we'll send a short neighborhood guide with our recommendations — where to eat, where to stay, and where to gather the night before.

The setup

How the picnic is set up.

Cathedral Park is bigger than it looks, and not every corner of it feels the same. There's one spot we'd choose for ourselves — quieter, more tucked away, with picnic tables set close together, each seating up to 10 — and it's the one we reserve for you. The Garden Party includes one of those tables and The Soirée includes two. Your table is the anchor of the whole setup: florals, charcuterie, candles, and place settings share the surface. For both tiers, a third table is styled separately for the dessert spread, so sweets have a home of their own and the main table stays comfortable.

The sundries

A generous spread from two St. Johns kitchens.

Charcuterie, fruits, nuts, and breads, all sourced and arranged by She Serves, with a dessert spread from Flower & Flour. Vegan options available.

Beverages come from our neighborhood partners: your choice of Oregon wine, local craft beer, or a mix of both, alongside seasonally infused water from a drink dispenser. Everything is laid out and ready before you return from portraits.

Linen tablecloths, candlelight, and perfectly mismatched place settings. Florals from Still City Flowers woven in among the food and candles. Fruits, nuts, and breads nestled between arrangements. The kind of table that looks like it took no effort at all.

Permits & park rules

Cathedral Park is a public park, and we treat it like one.

Your picnic reception is fully permitted, and the permit fee is included in your package. Because the permit has to be held in your name as host, there's one short application only you can submit — we send you everything you need and remind you well ahead of the deadline. If it isn't submitted in time, your reception moves indoors to the Weddinglandia chapel instead, so it's worth doing early.

Because the permit is in your name, you are the host and the alcohol is yours. Your package includes a beverage allowance, and we buy your beer and wine for you at cost with no markup, a straight pass-through with receipts available. It arrives sealed for you to serve. The park allows no glass, so cans or plastic only, and no hard alcohol or spirits.

Cathedral Park stays open to the public throughout, so expect a little life around you — walkers, dogs, someone else admiring the St. Johns Bridge. When the afternoon is over, we pack out everything we brought in and leave the park exactly as we found it.

Weather policy

We plan for Portland weather so you don't have to.

This package is designed for Portland's temperate seasons. A little rain or an overcast sky is not a reason to cancel. We have a clear rain plan in place and you will always know what is happening early on your wedding day.

A light rain or overcast sky is part of the experience, not a problem to solve.

If conditions call for it, the decision to move indoors is made by Higher Love Event Co. by 10:00am. You will be notified right away.

Rain plan

Your picnic moves to Weddinglandia.

If it rains, the picnic reception moves indoors to the Weddinglandia chapel on N. Burlington Avenue, a few minutes from the park. Your ceremony, florals, food, and everything included in your package stays exactly the same.

Rescheduling

Available for genuine safety concerns.

Packages are non-refundable and non-transferable. Rescheduling is available if conditions make travel or outdoor activity genuinely unsafe, such as a declared weather emergency or road closures.

Rain, clouds, or cooler temperatures on their own are not grounds for rescheduling.

Good to know

Questions couples ask us.

How many people can come to the ceremony?

That depends on where you'd like to say your vows, and it's your choice to make.

On the staircase, five: the two of you, your officiant, and two witnesses. The staircase is one of Portland Parks' official wedding venues, and reserving it takes a wedding permit that only you can apply for. Without one you can still marry there as a simple ceremony, but five is the limit and everyone counts, children included. That part isn't something we can flex. Everyone else waits at the picnic and sees you walk up married.

At a reserved spot elsewhere in the park, everyone in your package. That part of the park is covered by the special use permit we hold for your reception, which allows up to 30 people at the ceremony. That's more than any of our packages hold, so your whole guest list can watch, children included. There's no seating, so they stand, and then you all walk over to the picnic together.

You tell us which one 10 days before your date. Both options are laid out here.

When should we tell our guests to arrive?

If you're doing the staircase ceremony, 4:30 PM. Not 3:00 — that's your ceremony time, and it's just for the five of you.

We'd suggest putting Reception at 4:30 PM, Cathedral Park on your invitations and leaving the ceremony time off entirely. It saves a lot of explaining, and it means nobody turns up early hoping to watch.

If you've chosen a reserved spot in the park, your guests are invited to the ceremony, so we'll confirm the arrival time with you and you can put that on the invitations instead.

What do we need to do about a marriage license?

Apply at any county clerk's office in Oregon — it doesn't have to be Multnomah. Licenses can be secured three days ahead, and same-day licenses are sometimes available depending on timing and courthouse availability. Bring it with you on the day and we complete the paperwork afterward.

If you arrive without a valid license we can still hold the ceremony, but the marriage won't be legal and the package is non-refundable — so please build this into your timeline.

Is there anything we have to do after we book?

One short phone call, shortly after you book. It takes about ten minutes, and it's the only piece of the permitting that needs your voice rather than ours.

We send you everything you need beforehand and remind you when it's time. Everything after that is handled on our end.
