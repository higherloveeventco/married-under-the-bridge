The experience

A Cathedral Park wedding experience.
A collaboration of St. Johns creatives.

Higher Love Event Co. and Weddinglandia both call St. Johns home. We are neighbors who love this community — the people, the St. Johns Bridge, the parks, the small town feel that has somehow survived in the middle of one of the busiest cities in the Pacific Northwest. This package is what happens when local businesses choose collaboration and community over competition.

Weddinglandia coordinates the elopement: just the couple, two witnesses, and the officiant. Higher Love coordinates the picnic reception for you and your favorite people, the vendor team, and all logistics. Wedding permits beneath the St. Johns Bridge are currently unavailable while repairs wrap up, but between the two of us we found a way to help you get Married Under the Bridge.

The rest of the day belongs to our neighbors. The photography, the florals, the charcuterie, the desserts, the wine and the beer all come from St. Johns and North Portland businesses — people we know by name and have worked with directly. One neighborhood, showing up for your wedding day.

How the day flows

Vows, Portraits, and a Picnic.

The structure of this package was designed so both of you can be completely present for every part of it. Vows in the afternoon, portraits in the golden light, then a picnic waiting when you return. The day is built to give you time alone together before the celebration begins.

The first hour and a half belongs to the two of you. Everyone else arrives at 4:30, to a table already set.
01
3:00 — 3:30 PM

Ceremony at Cathedral Park

A legal ceremony beneath the St. Johns Bridge with an ordained officiant, ceremony recording, and marriage license filing, coordinated by Weddinglandia. You apply for your Oregon marriage license in advance at any county clerk's office in the state and bring it with you — we complete the paperwork afterward. The ceremony beneath the St. Johns Bridge is limited to five: the two of you, your officiant, and two witnesses — the number a marriage legally requires. Everyone else joins you at the picnic.

02
3:30 — 4:30 PM

Portraits in the park

While the picnic is being set, you and your photographer walk Cathedral Park. Time alone together, just the two of you, before anyone else arrives.

03
4:30 — 7:30 PM

The picnic awaits

The picnic opens when your portraits are done. Linen-draped tables, blankets and cushions, florals, candles, a full spread from She Serves and desserts from Flower & Flour. For The Garden Party and The Soirée, your guests arrive at 4:30 to welcome you as newlyweds. You sit down and enjoy it.

The neighborhood

Make a weekend of it in St. Johns.

St. Johns is a small town that happens to sit inside Portland — a main street of breweries, wine bars, bakeries, and shops, a few minutes from the park. Your wedding is one afternoon, but the neighborhood is worth more than that.

The night before

Gather everyone early.

A rehearsal dinner, drinks, or just a table big enough for whoever made the trip. St. Johns has room for a group without the reservation gymnastics of downtown, and it puts your people in the neighborhood before the day begins.

The wedding day

The neighborhood comes to you.

Wine from 45th Parallel. Beer from StormBreaker Brewing and Occidental Brewing Company, brewed within a few minutes of the park. The flowers, the grazing spread, the desserts, the photography — all from businesses in St. Johns and North Portland. Every part of your table has a name and an address attached to it.

After

Keep the night going.

The park closes out in the early evening, but St. Johns doesn't. Walk up for dinner or a nightcap with whoever's still with you. Plenty of couples make it a tradition and come back on their anniversary.

Tell us where your guests are coming from and we'll send a short neighborhood guide with our recommendations — where to eat, where to stay, and where to gather the night before.

The setup

How the picnic is set up.

Cathedral Park has two picnic tables set close together, each seating up to 10. Both tables are yours for the afternoon. Those anchor the whole setup — florals, charcuterie, candles, and place settings share the surface. For The Garden Party and The Soirée, a third vintage-style table is styled separately for the dessert spread, so sweets have a home of their own and the main tables stay comfortable.

The sundries

A generous spread from two St. Johns kitchens.

Charcuterie, fruits, nuts, and breads, all sourced and arranged by She Serves, with a dessert spread from Flower & Flour. Vegan options available.

Beverages come from our neighborhood partners: your choice of Oregon wine, local craft beer, or a mix of both, alongside seasonally infused water from a drink dispenser. Everything is laid out and ready before you return from portraits.

The atmosphere

Abundant, leisurely, celebratory.

Linen runners, candlelight, and perfectly mismatched place settings. Florals from Still City Flowers woven in among the food and candles. Fruits, nuts, and breads nestled between arrangements. The kind of table that looks like it took no effort at all.

We use picnic tables rather than a ground spread because we believe every guest should be comfortable and included. Blankets and cushions are on the lawn for those who want to settle in closer to the ground. If you have any accessibility needs, please let us know and we will do everything we can to accommodate.

Permits & park rules

Cathedral Park is a public park, and we treat it like one.

Your picnic reception is fully permitted, and the permit fee is included in your package. Because the permit has to be held in your name as host, there's one short application only you can submit — we send you everything you need and remind you well ahead of the deadline. If it isn't submitted in time, your reception moves indoors to the Weddinglandia chapel instead, so it's worth doing early.

Because the permit is in your name, you are the host and the alcohol is yours. Your package includes a beverage allowance, and we buy your beer and wine for you at cost with no markup — a straight pass-through, receipts available. It arrives sealed for you to serve. The park allows no glass, so cans or plastic only, and no hard alcohol or spirits.

Cathedral Park stays open to the public throughout, so expect a little life around you — walkers, dogs, someone else admiring the St. Johns Bridge. When the afternoon is over, we pack out everything we brought in and leave the park exactly as we found it.

Weather policy

We plan for Portland weather so you don't have to.

This package is designed for Portland's temperate seasons. A little rain or an overcast sky is not a reason to cancel. We have a clear rain plan in place and you will always know what is happening early on your wedding day.

A light rain or overcast sky is part of the experience, not a problem to solve.

If conditions call for it, the decision to move indoors is made by Higher Love Event Co. by 10:00am. You will be notified right away.

Rain plan

Your picnic moves to Weddinglandia.

If it rains, the picnic reception moves indoors to the Weddinglandia chapel on N. Burlington Avenue, a few minutes from the park. Your ceremony, florals, food, and everything included in your package stays exactly the same.

Good to know

Questions couples ask us.

How many people can come to the ceremony?

Five: the two of you, your officiant, and two witnesses.

The deck and staircase beneath the St. Johns Bridge can normally hold a much larger wedding — but the permits for that have been unavailable since 2022. Until they come back, an elopement is the only way to marry beneath the bridge, and an elopement is limited to the people a marriage legally requires. That's five, and it isn't something we can flex, even by one.

The picnic area is separate, and that we can reserve. So everyone you love is still there. They're waiting at 4:30 with a table already set, and the first thing they see is the two of you walking up married.

When should we tell our guests to arrive?

4:30 PM. Not 3:00 — that's your ceremony time, and it's just for the five of you.

We'd suggest putting Reception at 4:30 PM, Cathedral Park on your invitations and leaving the ceremony time off entirely. It saves a lot of explaining, and it means nobody turns up early hoping to watch.

What do we need to do about a marriage license?

Apply at any county clerk's office in Oregon — it doesn't have to be Multnomah. Licenses can be secured three days ahead, and same-day licenses are sometimes available depending on timing and courthouse availability. Bring it with you on the day and we complete the paperwork afterward.

If you arrive without a valid license we can still hold the ceremony, but the marriage won't be legal and the package is non-refundable — so please build this into your timeline.

Is there anything we have to do after we book?

One short phone call, shortly after you book. It takes about ten minutes, and it's the only piece of the permitting that needs your voice rather than ours.

We send you everything you need beforehand and remind you when it's time. Everything after that is handled on our end.
